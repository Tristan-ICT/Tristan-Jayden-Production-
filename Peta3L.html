LUZON:

Mt Mayon Bicol
The name originates from the Bicolano word magayon,meaning "beautiful."
Bangui Windmills
The Bangui Wind Farm in Ilocos Norte is Southeast Asia's first wind farm.
Baguio
Baguio, the "Summer Capital of the Philippines", is a mountain resort city in Northern Luzon.

Pagsanjan falls Laguna
Is a stunning 91-meter (300 ft) waterfall located in Cavinti, Laguna. A great falls to visit for!

Rizal Park
Rizal Park, also known as Luneta Park, is a massive 58-hectare urban park in Manila, Philippines


VISAYAS:

Chocolate Hills

The Chocolate Hills in Bohol, Philippines, are a geological wonder consisting of 1,260 to 1,776 symmetrical, cone-shaped limestone mounds.
boracay island

Boracay is a 10.32-square-kilometer resort island in the Philippines, famous for its 4-kilometer, powdery-white "White Beach".
Magellan's cross

Magellan's Cross is a historic Christian landmark in Cebu City, Philippines. Planted by explorers under Ferdinand Magellan in 1521.

Osmena Peak

The area is locally known as the "Little Baguio of Cebu" due to its cool climate and vegetable farms. The summit provides panoramic views of Bohol and Negros Island on clear days.
Fort San Pedro

The oldest and smallest triangular bastion fort in the Philippines. Located in Plaza Independencia near the Cebu City pier area.


MINDANAO:


Sunken Cemetery in Camiguin

Originally an above-ground burial site for the Bonbon settlement, it submerged below sea level during the eruption of Mount Vulcan in the 1870s.
Katibawasan falls

Known as the island's tallest waterfall, it cascades into a chilly, emerald-green rock pool surrounded by lush ferns, large vines, and dense jungle.
Guiob Church Ruins

The Guiob Church Ruins, located in Barangay Bonbon, Catarman, Camiguin, are the remnants of a 16th-century Spanish-era church.

White Island

White Island is an uninhabited, powdery white sandbar located 1.4 kilometers off the northern coast of Mambajao, Camiguin, in the Northern Mindanao region of the Philippines.
Punta Isla Resort

Punta Isla Lake Resort is a popular getaway located on the shores of Lake Sebu, South Cotabato. Established in 1995, it is renowned for its iconic floating restaurant.
